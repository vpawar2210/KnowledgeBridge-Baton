## Reconciliation timing
The daily reconciliation export is generated after 18:30 IST because the upstream ledger closes at 18:00 IST.

## Dashboard disagreement
If the deploy dashboard and service dashboard disagree, trust the service dashboard for customer impact until telemetry catches up.

## Handover evidence
For a handover, include the exact command and the expected output; a screenshot alone is not enough.
