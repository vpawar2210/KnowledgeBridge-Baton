# Support Escalation Runbook

- Document ID: DOC-SUP-005
- Owner: Customer Operations
- Classification: Internal
- Version: 3.0

## Triage sequence
1. Confirm the customer, affected workspace and request identifier.
2. Check the current service status before asking the customer to repeat an action.
3. Review the case timeline and attach relevant evidence.
4. Assign a severity using the published impact criteria.

## Refund handling
Do not promise a refund until the payment record and case timeline have been checked.

## Handover quality
Record the current owner, next action, customer impact and a link to the evidence.
